Sandbox

The public sandbox lets you run connection checks against the bundled demo agent. Sessions are short-lived, scoped, and revocable.

Acceptable use

  • Do not attempt to access other sessions or credentials
  • Do not probe internal hostnames or gateway endpoints
  • Do not use the sandbox for load testing or automated scraping
  • Do not submit real user data or production payloads

Availability

The sandbox is provided as-is for evaluation. AgentMesh Labs may suspend or rate-limit access to protect the shared infrastructure.

Commercial use

Production and multi-agent deployments require a commercial license.
